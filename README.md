## ⚠️ Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement. ℹ️

- **Elasticsearch and OpenSearch are the two dominant open-source search engines** — **Elasticsearch uses Elastic License 2.0 / SSPL**, while **OpenSearch is Apache 2.0-licensed** and **AWS-backed** . **Meilisearch and Typesense offer the fastest developer experience** with **sub-50ms latency** .

- **Amazon Kendra charges $1.125/hour for Enterprise Edition** plus **$0.25 per 1,000 queries** . **Glean starts around $50K/year for 100 users** . **Algolia starts at $1/month for 10K search requests** .

- **Open-source search engines are not turnkey** — they require **deployment, index configuration, and ongoing maintenance** . **Elasticsearch and OpenSearch require JVM tuning and cluster management** . **Vector databases (Qdrant, Weaviate, Milvus) require embedding pipeline setup** . **Always validate search relevance and latency with a proof-of-concept** before production deployment . 🔍
